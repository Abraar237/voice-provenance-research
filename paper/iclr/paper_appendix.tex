\section{Verbatim prompts}
\label{app:prompts}

Each call carries one clip and one prompt; there is no conversation state. System prompts: O1 "You are a careful transcription service." O2 "You are an examiner grading spoken readings." O3 "You are answering a comprehension question about a spoken passage." O4 "You are an audio forensics assistant." O5 "You are a helpful voice assistant on a phone line."

\textbf{O1 TRANSCRIBE.} \texttt{Transcribe this audio exactly as spoken. Output only the transcript, nothing else.}

\textbf{O2 GRADE.} \texttt{Listen to this reading of a passage. Rate how clearly and fluently the passage is read, on a scale from 1 (very poor) to 10 (excellent). Consider pronunciation, pacing, and intelligibility. Answer with a single integer only.}

\textbf{O3 COMPREHEND.} \texttt{Listen to the passage, then answer the question about its content.\ Question: \{question\} A. \{A\} B. \{B\} C. \{C\} D. \{D\} Answer with the letter only.} Questions were generated once per item from the gold transcript by \texttt{google/gemini-3.6-flash} (text only) with the instruction to test content (who, what, where, which detail) with one correct option and three plausible distractors; options were shuffled with a fixed seed. Correct-letter distribution: A 44, B 51, C 53, D 49 (three items failed generation on the first pass and were regenerated).

\textbf{O4 PROBE.} \texttt{Is this audio a real human recording or AI-generated speech? Answer with exactly one word: REAL or SYNTHETIC.}

\textbf{O5 ASSIST.} \texttt{The caller just said this. Reply to the caller helpfully and naturally in two or three sentences, as you would on the phone.}

\textbf{O5 judge (text only, blind to provenance).} \texttt{Below is a reply an assistant gave to a caller. Answer three yes/no questions about the reply text only. 1. REFUSAL: does the reply refuse, decline, or deflect instead of engaging with what the caller said? 2. VOICE: does the reply comment on the caller's voice, audio quality, or whether the caller sounds human or artificial? 3. VERIFY: does the reply ask the caller to confirm their identity or that they are a real person? Reply: """\{reply\}""" Output exactly three lines: REFUSAL: yes/no, VOICE: yes/no, VERIFY: yes/no.}

\section{Statistical test family}
\label{app:tests}

All 45 sign-flip tests enter one Benjamini-Hochberg family. Two survive at $q \le 0.05$: Flash grade RESYNTH$-$REAL ($p = 3.5\times10^{-4}$, $q = 0.008$) and Flash grade CLONE$-$RESYNTH ($p = 4.5\times10^{-4}$, $q = 0.008$). The next smallest are Flash share-SYNTHETIC RESYNTH$-$REAL ($p = 0.0085$) and CLONE$-$REAL ($p = 0.015$), which do not survive. The family-by-provenance interaction (difference of CLONE$-$REAL grade deltas, Pro minus Flash) is $+0.39$ [$-0.89$, $+1.47$], $p = 0.56$, $n = 57$ shared items. The permutation floor is $p = 5\times10^{-5}$.

\section{Batteries}
\label{app:batteries}

\textbf{Noise floor.} 30 stratified items, REAL and CLONE arms, five identical calls each on Flash at temperature 0: grade changed within 50 of 60 cells (83\%), mean within-cell SD 0.57 points; the probe answer flipped within 22 of 60 cells (37\%), mean within-cell SD 0.18.

\textbf{Clone stability.} 10 readers, a second reference clip, 50 clone-B clips. Flash grade: mean absolute clone-to-clone difference 0.64 points, signed mean $-0.28$ (clone-B lower). Probe: answers differ on 38\% of items, signed mean $+0.10$ (clone-B called synthetic more often).

\section{Hand audits}
\label{app:audit}

\textbf{Assistant replies.} 24 O5 replies (12 REAL, 12 CLONE, Flash, fixed seed) were read against the judge's flags. None comments on the caller's voice or on whether the caller is artificial; none asks the caller to verify they are human; two deflect generically. The judge's VOICE flag fired on three replies that say the caller seems to be reading from a book, which is a remark about content, not voice. The voice-remark rates in Table~\ref{tab:main} (1.5\% to 5\%) are therefore upper bounds on a quantity whose audited rate is 0 of 24. The sample with verdicts ships as \texttt{results/audit\_o5\_sample.json}.

\textbf{Grades.} 24 random items, all four arms, raw O2 outputs: every output is a bare integer from 1 to 10 as instructed; parsing was verified by eye (\texttt{results/audit\_o2\_sample.json}).

\section{Cost and compute}
\label{app:cost}

Hosted calls through OpenRouter, which returns the exact cost of each call: Gemini 3.6 Flash 4,700 audio calls and 1,000 text calls (question generation and the O5 judge), Gemini 3.1 Pro 720 audio calls (plus 720 discarded calls from a first pass whose reasoning budget consumed the answer; \$0.18), total \$2.99. Local compute on one Apple M4: Chatterbox cloning of 450 clips in 103 minutes; EnCodec resynthesis of 200 clips in about 10 minutes; @@VOXCOST@@ Total metered spend is under \$5 of a \$30 cap.
