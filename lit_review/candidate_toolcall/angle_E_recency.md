# Angle E — Recency Sweep (Jan–Sep 2026)

Sweep date: 2026-09-24. Scope: papers/industry lit posted Jan 2026 – Sep 2026 near our candidate idea:
**a controlled measurement of SILENT-WRONG tool calls in voice agents** (a spoken entity — amount, digit string, date, time, quantity, name/spelled email — is mis-heard, the call is schema-valid, no validator fires, the agent asks nothing, the call executes with wrong semantics), **across three pipelines** (local Whisper → text LLM; audio-LLM-as-transcriber → text LLM; audio-native LLM calling tools), **crossed with acoustic conditions** (≥3 voices per accent, rate, noise, 8 kHz telephone), **with recovery levers compared** (read-back confirmation with a scripted oracle, ASR-confidence / n-best gating, ask-when-unsure instruction).

Sources used: arXiv API (queries "voice agent"+tool, "voice agent"+"function calling", speech+"function calling", "audio language model"+tool, spoken+"tool calling", "voice assistant"+confirmation, ASR+"tool call", "spoken dialogue"+clarification, etc., date-sorted); Semantic Scholar search API (year=2026) and the Semantic Scholar batch endpoint for abstracts; WebSearch for 2026 arXiv HTML pages and industry evals. **The arXiv API returned HTTP 429 for every call during this sweep (including a single `id_list` request), so every abstract below was fetched live through the Semantic Scholar batch endpoint (`ARXIV:<id>`), and four papers (τ-Elicitation, From Text to Voice, SpeechGym, VoiceCodeBench) were additionally read in full text on arxiv.org/html to delineate.** All 30 arXiv IDs listed were verified (title + abstract + date returned). ## EXACT-NICHE and NEAR entries: what they cover and what they leave to us

**arXiv 2609.13602 — τ-Elicitation (2026-09-11) — EXACT-NICHE.** This is the closest paper and it is two weeks old. It measures exact spoken-entity capture in voice agents across 10 entity types with five ElevenLabs caller voices, three environments (regular, 10 dB noise + bursts + frame drops + muffling, speech-heavy), scores first-captured and final-database values against gold, analyses whether agents verify wrong captures (GPT/Gemini verify 66%/64% of wrong captures vs 50% of correct ones) and how often verified errors are repaired (24–37%), and shows that a prescribed spell/read-back/confirm/correct scaffold raises robust Pass³ by 14–31 points at 21–28 s per call. That is our read-back lever, our entity-type breakdown and part of our calibration question. What it leaves: it evaluates only audio-native realtime models (GPT-Realtime-2, Gemini-3.1-flash-live, Grok-voice) and deliberately omits caller-side ASR "to avoid confounding agent performance with a second recognizer", so there is no cascade arm, no local Whisper, no audio-LLM-as-transcriber arm, and no ASR-confidence / n-best gating lever; it reports no WER covariate; it has no telephone band, no speaking-rate manipulation, and no accent × multiple-voices-per-accent design; and it does not report a formal SILENT-WRONG cell (schema-valid wrong value executed with no question asked) as a rate per entity type × condition, nor the lever's false-positive cost on calls that were already right. Our paper must cite it in the first paragraph and position itself as the cascade-vs-native, confidence-gating-vs-read-back, telephone-band, ledger-shaped complement.

**arXiv 2605.15104 — From Text to Voice (2026-05-14) — NEAR, borderline EXACT.** Converts Confetti and When2Call into paired audio with TTS (2 voices per provider), DEMAND noise at 5–20 dB SNR, and compares clean text, direct omni-model voice and an ASR-to-text cascade across 7 omni models; argument-value errors are the largest failure class (54–57%). It has the pipeline comparison and the noise sweep and it identifies argument values as the failure locus. It leaves: no recovery lever of any kind, no telephone band, no speaking rate, no accent design, no distinction between wrong-argument calls that executed silently and ones where the model asked (When2Call scores the decision, not recovery), and no entity-type breakdown (amount vs digit string vs date).

**arXiv 2608.26432 — SpeechGym (2026-08-26) — NEAR.** Audio-native τ²-bench self-play with Qwen3-Omni; quantifies mis-heard slot values in 32% of speech rollouts vs 2% in text and attributes voice failure to perception not reasoning; trains with a per-turn process reward. It leaves the measurement question entirely (one open model, clean self-play audio, no cascade, no acoustic factorial, no confirmation lever; repair behaviours emerge from RL rather than being measured as levers).

**arXiv 2608.28916 — VoiceCodeBench (2026-08-28) — NEAR.** Shows WER is nearly uncorrelated with exact entity recovery (ρ≈−0.2 to −0.3) across 19 ASR systems on 26 entity types including phone, currency, date, time, account number and spelled sequences. It is the ASR-side evidence for our D4 ("WER a poor predictor"). It leaves: no LLM, no tool call, no execution, no audio-LLM transcriber, clean human audio only, no noise or telephone conditions, no recovery.

**arXiv 2609.20152 — MTVA-Bench (2026-09-17) — NEAR.** Evaluates the text LLM inside a cascade with a mock backend that responds to the arguments actually sent, and finds argument values are a main driver of score spread. It leaves: the audio is simulated as transcript artefacts (no real ASR, no acoustic conditions), no entity-type ledger, no confirmation or gating lever.

**arXiv 2605.25404 — Proactive for Uncertainty (2026-05-25) — NEAR.** Argues ASR confidence filtering is a weak gate for cascaded SDS and replaces it with cause-aware detectors on ASR latents that trigger targeted clarification; +17% downstream task. It is a direct competitor to our confidence-gating lever, but it is an SDS/WER study, not a tool-call ledger: no schema-valid-wrong cell, no audio-native comparison, no read-back baseline with an oracle.

**arXiv 2605.29430 — Agentic / Interactive ASR (2026-05-28) — NEAR.** Multi-turn ASR refinement with semantic correction and a semantic error rate on entity-intensive sets. Leaves everything downstream of the transcript: no tool calls, no pipelines, no acoustic factorial, no lever cost accounting.

**arXiv 2607.27453 — VAmoS Bench (2026-07-29) — NEAR.** End-to-end phone simulation with real SQL tools whose grader checks arguments and rows, so it can catch a wrong database write. It leaves: 100 scenarios in one domain, no entity-type or acoustic factorial, no lever comparison, and it scores containment rather than a silent-wrong rate.

**arXiv 2606.21453 — CORTIS (2026-06-19) — NEAR.** Cascade vs SLM for speech→function-call outputs under acoustic degradation, but as a training method paper; no error-type ledger, no confirmation.

**arXiv 2604.04847 — Full-Duplex-Bench-v3, arXiv 2603.13686 — τ-Voice, arXiv 2604.22821 — Audio2Tool, arXiv 2608.05126 — SFC-Bench, arXiv 2608.06027 — FormBharo — NEAR.** Voice tool-use benchmarks (some with a Whisper cascade arm, accents, noise, or human audio) that report task pass rates. None reports a silent-wrong cell by entity type, none crosses pipeline with telephone band and voices-per-accent, and none compares recovery levers with their turn/token cost.

## Verdict

**One EXACT-NICHE hit (τ-Elicitation, arXiv 2609.13602, posted 2026-09-11) and one borderline-EXACT NEAR hit (From Text to Voice, arXiv 2605.15104).** τ-Elicitation already publishes the headline that a spell/read-back/confirm scaffold recovers a large share of spoken-entity failures at a measured seconds-per-call cost, with an entity-type breakdown, five caller voices and a noise condition, and it already observes that agents under-verify wrong captures. From Text to Voice already publishes the cascade-vs-audio-native tool-calling comparison under a noise sweep with argument-value errors as the dominant failure. SpeechGym independently quantifies mis-heard slot values (32% vs 2%) and VoiceCodeBench independently shows WER does not predict entity recovery.

**Still apparently open (our residual novelty):** the SILENT-WRONG ledger itself — the rate at which schema-valid, wrong-semantics calls execute with no error and no question, per entity type × pipeline (local Whisper cascade vs audio-LLM transcriber vs audio-native) × acoustic condition (voices per accent, rate, noise, 8 kHz mu-law) — with WER as a measured covariate, the clarification-calibration 2×2 (asked/silent × right/wrong), and a head-to-head of read-back confirmation vs ASR-confidence / n-best gating vs an ask-when-unsure instruction with recovery fraction, extra turns, tokens and false-positive cost. No 2026 paper found does that factorial or that lever comparison. But the niche is now crowded from four directions in the last five months, and τ-Elicitation occupies the read-back headline; the paper must be framed as "the cascade/native × acoustic ledger and the gating-vs-read-back comparison" rather than "read-back recovers spoken entities", must cite 2609.13602, 2605.15104, 2608.26432 and 2608.28916 in the introduction, and must move fast — a τ-Elicitation follow-up adding a cascade arm or a confidence gate would close the gap.
