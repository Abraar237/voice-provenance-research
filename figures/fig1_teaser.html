The clone is invisible. Grades, transcripts, comprehension, and assistant replies are the same for the clone and the real recording.
The codec is not. Re-encoding the real recording lowers its grade by a third of a point with no change in intelligibility.
Asked directly, the model leans synthetic. Most genuine recordings are called AI-generated; real vs clone is near chance.
